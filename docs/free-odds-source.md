# Free historical odds source

The project now supports Football-Data.co.uk as a free historical odds source. Its published CSV files include Premier League results and betting odds, including match and total-goals odds. The source states that its data files are free and are updated during the season.

Source: https://www.football-data.co.uk/data.php

For reproducibility, preserve the source URL, season code, retrieval date, and the exact columns used. Do not redistribute source data without reviewing the source's terms.
